Pathway 1 — Apply for Business Support
For Entrepreneurs & Businesses
Building a business with room to grow?
NYEIB supports growth-oriented businesses connected to Nigerian youth and women, with a focus on creating jobs and building stronger, more investable enterprises.
This pathway is designed for businesses with a working product or service, paying customers and a clear plan for growth.
Depending on your stage and needs, you may be considered for equity or quasi-equity investment, capacity-building support, or both.

   Before You Apply
   A simple first step.
   The initial application or partnership form is designed to take approximately five minutes.
   You only need to provide the information required for initial screening. If your submission progresses, the NYEIB team may request additional details or supporting documents.
   There is no application fee. NYEIB does not work through paid agents or intermediaries who charge applicants for access.
   What Happens Next?
   For Business Applications
   Step 1 — Submit your application.
   Complete the business application form with information about your enterprise, its operations, growth plans and the support you are seeking.
   Step 2 — Initial screening.
   Your submission will be reviewed against the relevant initial screening criteria.
   Step 3 — Further assessment.
   If your business progresses, the NYEIB team may request additional information or documentation to support the assessment process.
   For Partnership Enquiries
   Step 1 — Choose your stakeholder group.
   Select the category that best describes your institution or organisation.
   Step 2 — Share your interest.
   Complete the relevant form with your institution's details, mandate and proposed area of collaboration.
   Step 3 — Partnership review.
   The NYEIB team will review your submission and follow up where an appropriate opportunity for engagement exists.
   Important Information
   Every application is considered through the relevant review process.
   Submitting a business application or partnership enquiry does not guarantee funding, investment, grant support or an institutional partnership.
   All opportunities remain subject to applicable eligibility requirements, screening, assessment and approval processes.

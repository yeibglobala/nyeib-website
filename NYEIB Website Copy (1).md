# Page: What We Do

# Hero

**We exist to unlock pathways for investable businesses.**

NYEIB connect growth-oriented businesses with the capital, strategic partnerships and practical support they need to become more credible, resilient and investment-ready.

### Problem / Purpose

**Ambition was never the problem; access was.**

Across Nigeria, promising businesses are held back by financing gaps, limited business support and fragmented access to growth opportunities. NYEIB exists to help close those gaps.

### What Makes NYEIB Different?

**Built to expand access for sustainable growth.**

NYEIB combines financial and non-financial support within one platform designed to strengthen businesses and build confidence among the institutions that back them.

### Blended Capital

**Blended Capital**

We bring together equity and quasi-equity investment, risk-sharing guarantees, grants and reimbursable grants to address different financing needs.

### Growth-Oriented Focus

**Focused on Growth-Oriented Businesses**

We support promising businesses across sectors with the potential to scale, create jobs and generate sustainable economic value.

###

### Ecosystem Development

**Strengthening the Ecosystem**

We work with ecosystem organisations and business-development providers to strengthen the capabilities and support businesses need to grow and become investment-ready.

### Governance

**Governance designed to build confidence.**

Nigeria NYEIB Investment Funds combines professional management, independent oversight and clearly defined investment structures to support credible and accountable capital deployment.

### Institutional Relationships

**Institutionally anchored for long-term impact.**

NYEIB operates within an institutional framework involving the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority, Development Bank of Nigeria and African Development Bank.

### Impact

**Designed to create measurable economic impact.**

Over its 20-year horizon, NYEIB is designed to support approximately **38,400 businesses** and contribute to the creation of approximately **1.6 million direct and indirect jobs**.

### Closing CTA

**Partner with NYEIB**

Whether you are building a growth-oriented business, deploying capital or strengthening Nigeria’s entrepreneurial ecosystem, there is a pathway to work with NYEIB.

### Closing Brand Line

**The institutional ally at the meeting point of ambition and capital.**

# **Page: Impact & Measurement**

# **Hero**

### **Our 20-Year Targets**

**Measuring the outcomes that matter.**

Over the 20-year life of the Funds, NYEIB is targeting measurable outcomes across business support, job creation, gender inclusion and ecosystem development.

### **Jobs Created**

**\~1.6 million direct and indirect jobs**

Targeted over the 20-year life of the Funds, including approximately 166,000 direct jobs and 1.44 million indirect jobs.

### **Businesses Supported**

**\~38,400 businesses supported**

Expand financial and non-financial support to growth-oriented youth- and women-led businesses across Nigeria.

### **Ecosystem Support Organisations**

**118 ecosystem support organisations mobilised**

Strengthen organisations that provide business development, capacity-building and other services to MSMEs.

###

###

### **Gender Parity**

**Gender parity by Year 5**

We aim to achieve gender parity as part of our commitment to stronger gender integration across the Funds.

### **How We Measure Impact**

We assess performance across three areas: **Impact, Additionality and Risk.**

### **Impact**

**Are businesses and communities better off because of our intervention?**

We track outcomes such as jobs, business growth, productivity and wider economic benefits.

### **Additionality**

**Are we unlocking capital that would not otherwise be available?**

We assess whether NYEIB brings new capital into the market, expands access to finance and encourages more institutions to back youth- and women-led businesses.

### **Risk**

**Are we creating impact while protecting capital responsibly?**

We monitor financial, portfolio, liquidity, macroeconomic, reputational and ESG risks across the Funds.

### **Accountability**

**Tracking progress as we grow.**

As capital is deployed, we will monitor performance against our impact framework and use what we learn to strengthen implementation over time.

###

### **Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority**

**Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority (NSIA)**

An in-country sponsor and shareholder within the NYEIB structure.

### **Development Bank of Nigeria**

**Development Bank of Nigeria (DBN)**

An in-country sponsor and shareholder, with the credit-guarantee mechanism structured through DBN’s Impact Credit Guarantee Limited.

### **African Development Bank**

**African Development Bank (AfDB)**

A key institutional partner supporting the establishment of NYEIB, including the sovereign financing underpinning its initial capitalisation.

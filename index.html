How we measure impact

Impact. Additionality. Risk.

Three connected areas help us understand whether the Funds are creating value responsibly.

01 / IMPACT

Are businesses and communities better off?

We track outcomes such as jobs, business growth, productivity and wider economic benefits.

02 / ADDITIONALITY

Are we unlocking capital that would otherwise be unavailable?

We assess whether NYEIB brings new capital into the market, expands access to finance and encourages more institutions to back youth- and women-led businesses.

03 / RISK

Are we creating impact while protecting capital responsibly?

We monitor financial, portfolio, liquidity, macroeconomic, reputational and ESG risks across the Funds.

Accountability

Tracking progress as we grow.

As capital is deployed, we will monitor performance against our impact framework and use what we learn to strengthen implementation over time.

Transparent by design

Performance monitoring helps NYEIB assess outcomes, identify areas for improvement and communicate development progress over time.

Institutional strength

Institutionally anchored. Professionally structured.

NYEIB is being established through an institutional framework involving the following partners, with dedicated governance, investment and risk-management arrangements.

In-country sponsor

Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority (NSIA)

An in-country sponsor and shareholder within the NYEIB structure.

In-country sponsor

Development Bank of Nigeria (DBN)

An in-country sponsor and shareholder, with the credit-guarantee mechanism structured through DBN’s Impact Credit Guarantee Limited.

Institutional partner

African Development Bank (AfDB)

A key institutional partner supporting the establishment of NYEIB, including the sovereign financing underpinning its initial capitalisation.

NYEIB operates within a broader institutional framework involving relevant government, regulatory and public-sector stakeholders.

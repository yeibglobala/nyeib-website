## Our Foundation

### Who We Are
Nigeria YEIB Investment Funds is the institutional ally positioned at the meeting point of ambition and capital. We serve both sides of the investment ecosystem: youth- and women-led businesses seeking the funding, partnerships and practical support required to grow, and institutional investors seeking credible, well-governed opportunities that deliver measurable impact and sustainable returns.

Through equity investment, credit guarantees, strategic partnerships and full business-cycle support, we help promising enterprises become stronger, more resilient and investment-ready. At the same time, we reduce risk, strengthen accountability and build the evidence institutions need to deploy capital with confidence.

Our role extends beyond funding businesses; we create the conditions for Nigerian founders to scale sustainably, generate employment and demonstrate that youth- and women-led enterprises are a commercially viable asset class capable of driving national economic growth.

### Purpose
**To create a bankable future for Nigerian youth and women.**

MSMEs are the backbone of Nigeria's economy, contributing 48% of GDP and generating 84% of employment. Behind most of these businesses are young Nigerians and women entrepreneurs who have the ideas, the drive, and the determination to build.

When they are de-risked and given the capital, access, and ecosystem they need to grow, they do not just build businesses, they build lives worth staying for. They invest back into the communities around them, create jobs for other women and young Nigerians, while becoming the kind of thriving entrepreneurs that make Nigeria increasingly attractive to foreign investors.

We are committed to giving every growth-minded woman and youth the support they need to build, sustain, and own their future here.

### Promise
**Unlocking Pathways for Investable Businesses**

* **For youth- and women-led MSMEs:** Our promise is access. We provide support that grants them access to the capital, opportunities, and support they need to grow, become more credible and investable.
* **For intermediaries, commercial banks, and MFIs:** Our promise is confidence and reduced risk. We remove the obstacles that make youth and women-led businesses risky to back, so they can invest with confidence.
* **For institutional investors and partners:** Our promise is economic value. We create a pathway to businesses with high growth potential, measurable impact, and long-term economic value.

### Vision
To create a future where Nigerian youth and women are seen as an investable asset class driving economic growth.

We are working toward a future where Nigerian youth and women are no longer seen as too risky or too inexperienced, but as one of the country's strongest and most investable asset classes. A future where commercial banks compete to lend to them, institutional investors actively seek them out, and the talents that left for greener pastures abroad choose to return and build here.

### Mission
We provide an ecosystem where youth and women-led MSMEs can access the capital, partnerships, and support they need for sustainable business growth.

We exist to help business growth more possible, accessible, and sustainable for young and women entrepreneurs by providing capital, strategic partnerships, and practical support.

### Positioning Statement
For youth- and women-led MSMEs, institutional investors, and financial institutions, Nigeria YEIB Investment Funds is the preferred ecosystem that facilitates promising businesses to become investable assets by providing full business cycle support through capital, access and strategic partnerships.

## Verbal Identity

### Tone of Voice
Our verbal identity is how we sound, speak, write, and presents ourselves across every communication touchpoint. This shapes how entrepreneurs, investors, financial institutions, ecosystem partners, and the wider public understand who we are, what we stand for, and why our work matters.

YEIB operates in a space where trust is critical. Youth- and women-led MSMEs need to believe that access is possible. Investors and financial institutions need to believe that these businesses are investable. Ecosystem partners need to understand their role in building a stronger pipeline of sustainable enterprises. As a result of this, our communication must be clear, credible, and human.

* **Confident**
* **Inspiring**
* **Honest**

#### 1. Confident
We speak with certainty because we know what we stand for and what the evidence shows. We do not exaggerate, oversell, or hide behind buzzwords. We make claims we can back with data and evidence.
* **We are:**
  * **Assured:** We understand our mandate and we use clear language to communicate it—our role, commitment, and point of view.
  * **Factual:** We use evidence, data, market realities, and context to support what we say.
  * **Thorough:** We do not provide shallow or half-way support as we are committed to giving youth and women-led MSMEs the ecosystem support they need to become investable and sustainable.
* **We are not:**
  * **Presumptuous:** We do not speak on what we do not know or make claims we cannot support.
  * **Delusional:** We do not speak as though we are out of touch with the realities of youth- and women-led MSMEs, or as though every business is already investment-ready and ambition alone guarantees success.
  * **Dismissive:** We do not downplay the barriers entrepreneurs face or the concerns financial institutions and investors may have.

#### 2. Inspiring
We speak in a way that helps our audience believe in what is possible. Our audiences are building businesses, taking risks, and navigating systems that have not always made growth easy. Our voice should help them see that progress is possible when ambition is met with the right capital, support, partnerships, and structure.
* **We are:**
  * **Optimistic:** We use positive language that helps our audiences believe in the possibility of growth, investment readiness, and long-term business success.
  * **Experienced:** We draw from YEIB's wider institutional experience in entrepreneurship, ecosystem strengthening, and inclusive economic development to speak with depth.
  * **Insightful:** We understand the economic landscape, the barriers youth- and women-led MSMEs face, and the trust gaps that exist. Our communication shows that we are not asking them to try again blindly; we are pointing them towards a more structured pathway to growth.
* **We are not:**
  * **Pessimistic:** We acknowledge the barriers, but we do not speak as though they are impossible to overcome.
  * **Performative:** We do not use motivational language that sounds good but says little.
  * **Patronising:** We do not speak to youth- and women-led MSMEs as helpless beneficiaries. We speak to them as capable builders who need the right support to grow.

#### 3. Honest
We say what we mean and mean what we say. We do not over-promise, hide behind jargon to sound impressive. Our audience has been let down before. We earn their trust by being straightforward, transparent, and human.
* **We are:**
  * **Transparent:** We communicate our processes, criteria, results, and impact clearly. We do not publish inflated reports or make claims for public validation.
  * **Credible:** We do not partner with questionable or manipulative institutions that may exploit the businesses we exist to support.
  * **Fair:** We set clear criteria. We do not discriminate by tribe, gender, location, personal connections, or influence once an applicant meets the required standards.
* **We are not:**
  * **Misleading:** We do not create false expectations around funding, eligibility, timeliness, or business outcomes.
  * **Exploitative:** We do not use the struggles or ambitions of youth- and women-led MSMEs as emotional bait.
  * **Biased:** We do not make access feel reserved for people with influence, proximity, or personal connections.

### Archetypes
* **Primary Archetype: The Hero**
  Nigeria YEIB Investment Fund embodies the hero archetype by existing to make the businesses it backs the ones who triumph. The fund rises to the challenge of a broken capital landscape, channelling courage and competence to create an ecosystem, capital, and systems that make self-sufficiency possible for youth and women-led enterprises. We are resilient, disciplined, excellence-driven, accountable, fearless, conviction-led.
* **Secondary Archetype: The Everyman**
  The Everyman archetype is rooted in belonging and the belief that everyone deserves a fair shot—regardless of status, background, or geography. We carry this by building an institution that is accessible to every qualifying founder. We believe that every youth and woman deserves a fair opportunity to build, grow, and scale. We are approachable, trustworthy, genuine, empathetic, inclusive.

### Personality & Culture
* **Authoritative:** Speaks with the weight of sovereign-anchored institutions and published evidence.
* **Resilient:** Stays the course through tough economic cycles, sceptical institutions, and businesses finding their footing.
* **Approachable:** Warm, clear, and easy to engage with; fair and accessible.
* **Responsible:** Commanded confidence through clarity and mandate understanding.
* **Excellent:** Highest standards from capital deployment to communications.
* **Teamwork:** Moving as one toward a shared vision.

### Naming Convention
* **Brand Name:** Always refer to the brand as **Nigeria YEIB Investment Funds** in formal communication, official documents, investor materials, press releases, stakeholder presentations, reports, and first mentions.
* **Shorthand:** **YEIB** or **NYEIB** may be used after full introduction or due to space constraints. Always capitalised. Never lower-cased, pluralised, hyphenated, or broken into separate initials (e.g., never *Yeib*, *yeib*, *YEIBS*, *Y-E-I-B*, *Y.E.I.B*).

## Visual Identity

### Ecosystem + Access
Our visual identity maps our ecosystem as a defined pathway a business can follow toward sustained success.

### Logo Architecture
* **Primary Logo Signature:** Features the stylised "Y" ascending access path along with the full name "NIGERIA YEIB INVESTMENT FUNDS".
  * *Three distinct streams:* Demand (founders/MSMEs), Supply (institutions/financiers), and Ecosystem Supporters (policymakers/enabling partners).
* **Abridged Logo:** Combines the logo mark with the shortened name "NIGERIA YEIB". Used only when primary logo context is established.
* **Vertical Logo Signature & Vertical Abridged:** Compact expressions for extreme space constraints (e.g., key rings, avatars).
* **Logo Mark:** Stands alone ($Y$). Defined geometry communicates stability, direction, and institutional confidence.

### Clear Space & Minimum Sizes
* **Clear space:** Equal to the size of the "Y" in YEIB (except Logo Mark which requires $1/3 \times$ the height of the logo mark).
* **Minimum Sizes:**
  * Primary Signature: Digital: 160 px | Print: 40 mm
  * Abbreviated/Vertical Signatures: Digital: 120 px | Print: 30 mm
  * Vertical Logo Abridged: Digital: 80 px | Print: 20 mm
  * Logo Mark: Digital: 24 px | Print: 8 mm

### Grid System & Layout System
* Defined for Instagram Portrait (80 px margin, 9-column), Instagram Square (100 px margin, 7-column), A4 Portrait (1/12th width margin, 9-column), and A4 Landscape.
* **Layout Splits (Portrait & Landscape):** Utilises 70-30 splits, 50-50 splits, and Diagonal splits governed by precise scaling factors ($1.76\times$ or $1.05\times$ canvas dimensions) using curved logo sweep vectors as construction bounds.

## Colour Palette & Typography

### Colour Palette
* **Evergreen:** HEX `#003124` | RGB `0, 49, 36` (Foundational trust, stability, long-term growth)
* **Mint Leaf:** HEX `#00BE93` | RGB `0, 190, 147` (Freshness, innovation, forward momentum)
* **Pale Oak:** HEX `#E1C9B3` | RGB `225, 201, 179` (Warmth, balance, refined character)
* **Mint Cream:** HEX `#F2FBF6` | RGB `242, 251, 246` (Calm light neutral for clarity)
* **Tiger Orange:** HEX `#F88404` | RGB `248, 132, 4` (Energetic accent representing ambition and opportunity)

### Typography System
* **Headlines:** **Asul** (Bold/Regular, minimum scale 20pt) - expressive humanist letterforms for distinctive authoritative presence.
* **Body Copy:** **Chivo** (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold with italics) - clean sans-serif structure providing excellent legibility.

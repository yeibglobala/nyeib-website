Page: What We Do
Hero
We exist to unlock pathways for investable businesses.
NYEIB connects growth-oriented businesses with the capital, strategic partnerships and practical support they need to become more credible, resilient and investment-ready.
Problem / Purpose
Ambition was never the problem; access was.
Across Nigeria, promising businesses are held back by financing gaps, limited business support and fragmented access to growth opportunities. NYEIB exists to help close those gaps.
What Makes NYEIB Different?
Built to expand access for sustainable growth.
NYEIB combines financial and non-financial support within one platform designed to strengthen businesses and build confidence among the institutions that back them.
Blended Capital
Blended Capital
We bring together equity and quasi-equity investment, risk-sharing guarantees, grants and reimbursable grants to address different financing needs.
Growth-Oriented Focus
Focused on Growth-Oriented Businesses
We support promising businesses across sectors with the potential to scale, create jobs and generate sustainable economic value.

Ecosystem Development
Strengthening the Ecosystem
We work with ecosystem organisations and business-development providers to strengthen the capabilities and support businesses need to grow and become investment-ready.
Governance
Governance designed to build confidence.
Nigeria NYEIB Investment Funds combines professional management, independent oversight and clearly defined investment structures to support credible and accountable capital deployment.
Institutional Relationships
Institutionally anchored for long-term impact.
NYEIB operates within an institutional framework involving the Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority, Development Bank of Nigeria and African Development Bank.
Impact
Designed to create measurable economic impact.
Over its 20-year horizon, NYEIB is designed to support approximately 38,400 businesses and contribute to the creation of approximately 1.6 million direct and indirect jobs.
